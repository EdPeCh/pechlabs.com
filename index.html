The AIgents

Five operations quietly killing growing businesses. Five AI workers that fix them.

01

Lead-Response AIgent

The lead you already paid for — going cold.

Replies to every inbound lead in seconds, any hour, qualifies with a few smart questions, and books the meeting or flags the hot ones to you.

02

Follow-Up AIgent

The follow-up that stops after one try.
